CRRT Sodium Kinetics Simulator

Parameters

Total body water. Acts as "inertia"—larger volumes slow the rate of sodium change.
L
The patient's starting serum sodium concentration prior to the simulation.
mEq/L
Continuous IV infusion rate of saline (e.g., 3% Hypertonic Saline).
mL/hr
Concentration of the infused fluid. Standard 3% Hypertonic Saline is ~513 mEq/L.
mEq/L
Total CRRT effluent flow rate. Determines how fast the patient's sodium exchanges with the dialysate.
L/hr
Sodium concentration of the CRRT fluid bags. Often customized by the pharmacy.
mEq/L
Net fluid removal rate. Does not change the steady state, but accelerates sodium changes as volume shrinks.
mL/hr
Add other concurrent fluids (e.g., normal saline, bicarbonate drips). The model automatically combines all infusions for a total sodium mass balance calculation.
Target Steady State (Na_ss) -- Flow-weighted average
Zero Net UF (Exponential) Na(t) = Na_ss + (Na₀ - Na_ss) × e-((R+k)/V)t
Active Net UF (Power-Law) Na(t) = Na_ss + (Na₀ - Na_ss) × (1 - Qnet·t/V)(R+k)/Qnet
